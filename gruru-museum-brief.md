# GRURU MUSEUM — Project Brief & Workspace Rule
## Theme: “New Knowledge. New Storytelling. Built for Everyday Living.”

### 1. Project Overview & Essence
- **Brand Concept**: Future Archaeology (“ห้องแล็บของนักโบราณคดีจากอนาคต”)
- **Atmosphere**: Modern Art Gallery (White Cube) x Sci-Fi Laboratory
- **Core Essence**: “อดีตคือข้อมูล อนาคตคือเรื่องเล่า” (Past is Data, Future is Story)
- **Brand Archetype**: “ภัณฑารักษ์จากอนาคต” (The Curator from the Future) — Smart, witty, fact-checked.
- **Brand Promise**: Every visit yields at least 1 Everyday Takeaway applied in real life.

### 2. The 3 Core Pillars
1. **New Knowledge**: Verified facts, rigorous provenance, zero hallucinations.
2. **New Storytelling**: Interactive visuals, graphs, and concise narratives in 3–5 minutes instead of lengthy essays.
3. **Built for Everyday Living**: Concludes with actionable takeaways for modern daily living.

### 3. The 5-Step Pipeline (The Excavation Engine)
1. **Excavate**: Curate and preserve historic, scientific, and cultural knowledge with 100% source citations.
2. **Decode**: Convert raw knowledge into entities (nodes) and semantic relationships (edges).
3. **Retell**: Visual digital storytelling digestible within 3 minutes.
4. **Connect**: Explore multi-source knowledge through Data Booming and Interactive Graph.
5. **Apply**: Conclude with Everyday Takeaway cards for daily application.

### 4. Target Personas (Age 22–40)
- **Curious Starter (22–27)**: Mobile-first, visual seeker, needs 3-min reads and 1080x1350 social knowledge cards.
- **Creative Professional (25–34)**: Designer/marketer/developer, cross-domain mental models, force graph deep dive & data exports.
- **Lifelong Learner (30–40)**: Fact-checking focus, wellness/wisdom seeker, needs clear actionable everyday living takeaways.

### 5. Design System & Tokens
- **Color Palette (70-20-10 Rule)**:
  - Base: Void Black (`#0B0B0F`), Lab Graphite (`#17171F`), Grid Line (`#2C2C3A`), Muted Fog (`#8A8A9E`), Archive Paper (`#F2EDE3`).
  - Signature Accents: Specimen Lime (`#D7FF3A`), Ember Orange (`#FF5A1F`), Quantum Violet (`#8C6BFF`), Hologram Cyan (`#2EE6FF`).
  - Time Warp Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF5A1F, #8C6BFF)`.
- **6 Category Shapes & Colors**:
  1. Science: Cyan (`#2EE6FF`) — Circle
  2. History: Ember (`#FF5A1F`) — Hexagon
  3. Technology: Violet (`#8C6BFF`) — Square
  4. Culture & Wisdom: Amber (`#FFC233`) — Triangle
  5. Nature & Life: Bio Green (`#34E89E`) — Diamond
  6. Art & Design: Coral (`#FF5FA2`) — Ring
- **Typography**: Chakra Petch (Headings), IBM Plex Sans Thai (Body), IBM Plex Mono (Specimen & Code).
- **HUD Chamfer**: 45° cut corners (12–14px clip-path).
- **Specimen Label Syntax**: `GRM-[YEAR]-[CAT]-[SEQ]` (e.g. `GRM-1872-SCI-014`).
- **Accessibility**: 100% WCAG AA compliant on Void Black.

### 6. Interactive Graph Engines
- **Data Booming**: Radial bursts from a central seed node, 40ms stagger, lime pulse wave, 3-layer drill-down, live connections counter.
- **Interactive Knowledge Graph**: Canvas/D3 force simulation, filtering by 6 categories, era slider, search, and Specimen Label HUD inspector with Everyday Takeaway.
